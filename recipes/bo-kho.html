Bo Kho Recipe

Ingredients

Marinade

  • 5tbsp Fish Sauce
  • 3tsp 5 Spice Powder
  • two inch Pieces of Ginger, sliced
  • 5 cloves of Garlic, minced

Braising Ingredients

  • 4lb Shank
  • 7 Carrots
  • 2 Onions sliced
  • 3 two inch Pieces of Ginger
  • 5 cloves of Garlic, minced
  • 7 Star Anise
  • 2 Bay Leaf
  • 2tsp Paprika
  • Dark Soy Sauce
  • Tomato Paste
  • Fish Sauce
  • 4 Lemongrass Stalks
  • 1 can of Rico Coconut Soda

Remaining Ingredients & Garnish

  • Thai Basil
  • Lime
  • Cilantro
  • Green Onion
  • French Baguette

Instructions

  1. Marinate Shank with 5tbsp fish sauce, 3tsp of 5 spice powder, ginger (sliced), and garlic (sliced) for 30-60 mins in the fridge.
  2. Sear shank on all sides over medium high heat and set aside to cool. Cut into 1-1.5 inch cubes.
  3. Saute onions for 2 mins. Add lemongrass, minced ginger, and minced garlic. Saute for another 2 mins.
  4. Add 2L water, 1 can of Rico Coconut Soda, 2tsp paprika, 4tbsp tomato paste, and 2 bay leafs. Mix well.
  5. Bring to simmar and cook for 2 hours.
  6. Add carrots towards the end (last 30 mins of cook time).
  7. Season with Dark Soy Sauce, salt, and fish sauce to taste.

For reference, I added 1tbsp dark soy sauce, 1tbsp salt, and 1tbsp tomato paste in last adjustment.

Serving

  • Serve in a large bowl garnished with thai basil, lime, cilantro, and green onion.
  • Eat together with french baguette.
